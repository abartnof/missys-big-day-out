## Music

All melodies are public domain, arranged for this game:

- Camptown Races, Stephen Foster (1850)
- Pop Goes the Weasel, traditional (1850s)
- The Entertainer, Scott Joplin (1902)
- William Tell Overture finale, Gioachino Rossini (1829)
- In the Hall of the Mountain King, Edvard Grieg (1875)
- Brahms' Lullaby, Johannes Brahms (1868)
- Home! Sweet Home!, Henry Bishop (1823)
- Shave and a Haircut (1899)

Every sound is synthesized live in the browser with the Web Audio API
(`src/dog-audio.js`, `src/audio.js`); there are no audio files.
